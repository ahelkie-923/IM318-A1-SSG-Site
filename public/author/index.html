Tatsuki Fujimoto

The writer and artist behind Chainsaw Man, known for fast, unpredictable stories that borrow as much from film as from manga.

Career

Fujimoto began publishing in the mid-2010s and gained attention with Fire Punch, a bleak and unusual story serialized online. It showed the mix of shock, humor and emotion that would define his later work.

In 2018 he launched Chainsaw Man in Weekly Shonen Jump. The series became an international hit and led to an anime adaptation by MAPPA, with a follow-up film after it.

Alongside his serialized work he has published acclaimed one-shots, including Look Back and Goodbye, Eri. Each shows a quieter and more personal side of his storytelling.

Fujimoto often frames scenes like a film director, using unusual angles, long quiet beats and sudden shifts in tone. Violence and comedy sit side by side, and big plot turns tend to arrive without warning.
