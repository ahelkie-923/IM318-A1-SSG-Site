        <h2 id="career-title">Career</h2>
        <div class="columns-2">
            <p>Fujimoto began publishing in the mid-2010s and gained attention with Fire Punch, a bleak and unusual story serialized online. It showed the mix of shock, humor and emotion that would define his later work.</p>
            <p>In 2018 he launched Chainsaw Man in Weekly Shonen Jump. The series became an international hit and led to an anime adaptation by MAPPA, with a follow-up film after it.</p>
            <p>Alongside his serialized work he has published acclaimed one-shots, including Look Back and Goodbye, Eri. Each shows a quieter and more personal side of his storytelling.</p>
            <p>Fujimoto often frames scenes like a film director, using unusual angles, long quiet beats and sudden shifts in tone. Violence and comedy sit side by side, and big plot turns tend to arrive without warning.</p>
        </div>
    </div>
</section>
